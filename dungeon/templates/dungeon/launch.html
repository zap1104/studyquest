How a run works

  • Walk the room with the arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen pad.
  • Tall grass hides enemies. Step into it and something may jump you.
  • Each enemy asks {{ rules.questions_per_enemy }} questions from your quiz. A right answer wounds it; a wrong one costs you {{ rules.damage_per_wrong_answer }} heart{{ rules.damage_per_wrong_answer|pluralize }}.
  • Every enemy you beat drops a key piece — and sometimes a potion.
  • Collect every piece to assemble the key, then walk out through the door for XP.
  • You start with {{ rules.base_player_hp }} hearts. Run out and the run ends where it stands.
